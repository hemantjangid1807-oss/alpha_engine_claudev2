A super alpha combines alphas you already submitted. The selection expression picks which ones, the combo expression weights them. Every selection is paired with every combo.

Which selection values count as "pick this alpha".
The most alphas one super alpha may combine.

Market, decay, neutralization and truncation come from Settings, and tick several decays or neutralizations to compare them. Results show up under Run with a SUPER tag, and you can check and submit them like any alpha.

The selection and combo syntax available to you is described in BRAIN's SuperAlpha documentation. A common start: give the alphas you want a shared name on BRAIN, select them by that name, and use a combo of 1 for equal weights.

Help

New to BRAIN? Start here.

Quick start

  1. Alphas. Paste expressions (one per line) or build many from a template.
  2. Settings. Keep the defaults, or pick another market. Tick several decay or neutralization values to compare them.
  3. Start simulating. Watch results arrive. Tap any result for details and a PnL chart.

Your settings, alpha list and history are saved inside the app automatically. When a run ends, its results are also saved as a CSV in Downloads/BrainAutoSim (Android 10 and newer). Use Share to send them anywhere.

What the numbers mean

Sharpe
Return per unit of risk. Higher is better; BRAIN usually wants above 1.25 to submit.
Fitness
Sharpe adjusted for returns and turnover. Usually needs above 1.
Turnover
How much the portfolio changes each day. Very high costs money, very low is suspicious.
Returns, Drawdown
Yearly profit, and the worst peak-to-trough loss.
Margin
Profit per dollar traded, in basis points of a basis point (‱).

Settings in plain words

Universe
The pool of stocks. TOP3000 is the 3,000 most traded US stocks.
Delay
1 means you trade the day after you see the data. That is the standard.
Neutralization
Cancels out market, sector or industry moves, so your idea has to stand on its own.
Decay
Averages the signal over recent days to reduce turnover.
Truncation
Caps how much one stock can weigh.

More ways to make alphas

Template Fill
Next to each {placeholder}, tap Fill. Use a ready-made list, save your own lists, or take every field of a BRAIN data category (filter by type, coverage and popularity). Synced fields download again by themselves when you change market. Pick "A random sample" to generate any number of expressions from huge combinations.
Generate with AI
Paste a key from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or an OpenAI-compatible service (OpenRouter, Groq, DeepSeek, Mistral...). Tap Load models to see what your key can use, choose the market and data, and say how many expressions you want.
Super alphas
Switch the Alphas screen to "Super alphas", write selection and combo expressions, and start. Every pairing is simulated with your Settings.
Check and submit
Tap any result. "Check submission" runs BRAIN's full checks including correlation; "Submit alpha" sends it to BRAIN after you confirm. Submitting can't be undone and BRAIN limits submissions per day.

Running on your phone

Screen off or another app
While a run is going, a notification shows its progress and the app keeps working in the background. Allow notifications when asked.
A run pauses in the background
Some phones close background apps to save battery. Set Brain AutoSim's battery use to "Unrestricted" (or "Don't optimise").
Back button
Closes the details panel, then goes back to your alpha list. Leaving the app during a run keeps it running.

If something goes wrong

"Incorrect email or password"
Use the same details as on the BRAIN website. No spaces at the end.
Biometric step
BRAIN may ask you to confirm your identity. The app opens the link in your browser; finish the check there, come back and tap "I've done it".
"unknown variable" error
That field doesn't exist for your region, universe or delay. Look it up in the Data explorer.
"limit reached" in the log
Normal. BRAIN limits parallel runs, so the app waits and retries. Lower Parallel simulations if it keeps happening.
Stop
Stop finishes what is already sent. Tap it twice to stop right now.
